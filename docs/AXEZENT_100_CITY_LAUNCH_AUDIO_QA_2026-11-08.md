## Important provenance and licensing

Collection Two uses the owner's 50 city-specific tempo, key, sound-palette and section-form blueprints and a deterministic General MIDI + FluidSynth rendering chain, producing **new full-length synthetic review mixes**. It is **not** an exact playback/export of the previously uploaded original MIDI-sketch ZIP, a professional multistem studio master, or a reproduction of any commercial rapper's protected musical recording. The site has no download button, but GitHub Pages audio endpoints remain technically retrievable on a public website. Any exclusive ownership sale requires confirming the precise rights and delivering appropriately cleared, artist-approved audio.

The first 50 new-review MP3s already published before expansion were **not regenerated** in this deployment; the existing public audio files were copied into the 100-city build.
