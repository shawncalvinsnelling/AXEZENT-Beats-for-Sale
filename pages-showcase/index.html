What you receive when you buy the beat

Intro→Hook→Verse 1→Hook→Verse 2→Outro

For an eligible AXEZENT beat, the proposed $100 exclusive purchase includes the expanded instrumental: intro, hook, verse one, hook, verse two, and outro. That extra verse gives the artist more room to record a complete song.

The longer instrumental and the specific rights transferred must be confirmed by AXEZENT in a signed written agreement before payment or delivery. These are the planned arrangements; each actual recording must match its stated structure before publication.

THE AXEZENT STANDARD

Made for the artist.

Each beat is planned as a complete original production, built with room for verses, memorable hooks and a compelling arrangement. Regional influences are creative directions, not claims that every city shares one signature sound.
